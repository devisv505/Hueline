Drag a belt from an ore deposit to a mixer. Two different colours go in, a new one comes out. Keep every shop supplied — and never let a mixer choke on a single colour.

How it works

Draw the line

  • Drag from a deposit or mixer onto a machine to lay a belt. Drag back over it to undo.
  • Tap a belt to pull it up and get the tiles back.
  • Belts cannot cross. Where one must hop another, a bridge is spent — you only get a few.
  • Mixers combine two different colours. Flood one with a single colour and the buffer overflows: it starts a countdown, then the whole line stops.
  • Tap a flashing machine to dump its buffer. It costs 5 from your score, but it saves the run.
