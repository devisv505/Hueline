Draw belts from ore to the hub. Each goal asks for one kind of bead — deliver enough and the next goal arrives, with a new machine to build it.

How it works

Draw the line

  • Drag from a deposit or machine onto another machine, or the hub, to lay a belt. Drag back over it to undo.
  • Tap a belt to pull it up and get the tiles back.
  • Belts can join other belts. Drop one onto a running lane and its load merges in, so two sources can share a line. Splitting a line still needs a splitter.
  • Belt tiles are your only limit. Every finished goal adds more, and pulling a belt up hands its tiles back.
  • Belts cannot cross. Where one must hop another it builds a bridge, which costs three extra tiles.
  • The hub wants one item at a time. Only that item counts, and anything else is scrapped at the door. Deliver the count and the next goal arrives, unlocking new buildings and adding belt tiles.
  • Mixers combine two different colours of the same size. Presses squeeze two matching items into one a size up: two beads make a pearl, two pearls an orb.
  • A machine that fills up with things it cannot use stops and flashes. Tap it to dump its buffer.
  • Pick a building from the bar under the floor, then tap an empty spot. Build as many as you like — on a keyboard the number keys pick them too. Press and hold a machine on the floor to move or remove it.
